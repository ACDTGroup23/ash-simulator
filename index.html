    Our measurements

    Barley grew in two 12-cell trays. Each column got 0, 1, 3 or 6 pinches of ash per cell (not weighed). Set 1 was sown on 23 Oct and Set 2 on 26 Oct. Bean did not germinate even in the controls, so it has no data.

    Ash level did not change barley height (two-way ANOVA on the 46 plants of 6 Nov: ash p = 0.47, set p < 0.001). Set 2 was 3.2 cm shorter than Set 1.

    Before vs after the experiment

    Before the experiment we only had published values, and two things were unknown: how salty our ash is and how much of its salt reaches the roots (f). In the model they always appear multiplied together, so we follow their product, the effective ash EC.

    After the experiment, the barley heights rule out the effective ash EC values that would have made the plants shorter. Linking our trays to Mg/ha needs one more number: the mass of one pinch of ash.
